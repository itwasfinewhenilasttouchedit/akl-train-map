About Hoki Trains

Hoki Trains shows Auckland's electric trains moving on a live map, using Auckland Transport's real-time data. It covers the Western, Eastern, Southern and Onehunga lines and Te Huia. Pick a station for upcoming departures, plan a trip, or see which carriages other riders are avoiding — a smell, a mess, crowding, rowdy behaviour, or something good. A six-carriage train is two halves joined, and a report tells you which half and which carriage, not just which train.

Every station has its own page, such as Waitemata (Britomart) and Newmarket, and so does every line, such as the Southern Line and the Eastern and Western Line.

Hoki — Auckland Trains

A live map of Auckland’s trains, with rider ratings on top. Open data from Auckland Transport; reports are from riders like you.

  • Track — see every train moving, delays, and when yours arrives at Home or Away.
  • Rate — tap a train and report a smell, crowding, damage, rowdy behaviour… or praise. Units are named like AM 701, with cars AMP, AMT and AMA.
  • Warn — reports colour the map and the planner so others can avoid a bad carriage.
